# The Hoyle State as Breathing Mode of Tetrahedral Vortex

## What Is the Hoyle State?

The 0₂⁺ excited state of ¹²C at 7.65 MeV is crucial for stellar nucleosynthesis — it allows carbon to form from three helium nuclei. Without it, the universe would have almost no carbon, and no life as we know it.

## Standard Interpretation

In nuclear physics, the Hoyle state is described as a "cluster state" — three α-particles (helium nuclei) loosely bound. But this doesn't explain why it exists at exactly 7.65 MeV.
