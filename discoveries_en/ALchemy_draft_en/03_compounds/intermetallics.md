# Intermetallics as New Entities

## What Are Intermetallics?

Intermetallic compounds are combinations of two or more metals that behave as distinct materials, not as mixtures. They have:
- Fixed stoichiometry
- Unique crystal structures
- Properties different from constituent metals

## In VMMS

Intermetallics are not "compounds" in the traditional sense. They are **new entities** with their own:
- Vortex number n
- Fractal level k
- Electronegativity χ
- Memory H

## Example: Cu₃Au

| Property | Copper | Gold | Cu₃Au |
|----------|--------|------|-------|
| Conductivity | High | High | Medium |
| Color | Red | Yellow | Red-gold |
| Structure | FCC | FCC | Ordered FCC |

Cu₃Au is not a mixture — it's a distinct phase with its own vortex structure.

## Why This Matters

In standard chemistry, intermetallics are treated as "exceptions". In VMMS, they are the rule:

The periodic table should include not only elements but also stable compounds.
