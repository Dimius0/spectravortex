# Alchemy as Precursor to VMMS

## The Forgotten Science

Before modern chemistry, alchemists described processes we now recognize as:
- Phase transitions
- Crystallization
- Alloy formation
- Purification
- Transformation of matter

They used symbolic language: sulfur, mercury, salt, the Philosopher's Stone.

## Key Figures

### Paracelsus (1493–1541)
Developed the tria prima: sulfur (soul), mercury (spirit), salt (body). In VMMS terms:
- Sulfur = energetic graviton flows
- Mercury = neutrino vortices
- Salt = condensed matter

### Isaac Newton (1643–1727)
Wrote more about alchemy than physics. His unpublished manuscripts describe:
- "Subtle matter" — precursor to the field H
- "Attraction and repulsion" — vortex interactions
- "Vegetation of metals" — growth of structures in H

### Jan Baptist van Helmont (1580–1644)
Coined the term "gas". Discovered that:
- Matter can transform into invisible forms
- Mass is conserved in chemical reactions
- "Spirit" (gas) is material, not magical

## What Alchemists Understood

- Matter is not static — it transforms
- Processes are more important than substances
- The observer participates in transformation
- "Imperfect" materials can become perfect
