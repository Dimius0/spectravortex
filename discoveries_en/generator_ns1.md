# NS-1: 1 kW Generator from Waste

## Proof of Concept

The NS-1 generator demonstrates Lipzik's Furcation Theorem in practice. By systematically violating conventional material protocols, a simple generator was built from recycled components.

## The Build

- **Materials**: Scrap metal, recycled copper wire, salvaged magnets
- **Protocol violations** (N=3):
  1. **Purity** — used mixed metals instead of pure conductors
  2. **Temperature** — operated outside standard thermal range
  3. **Field** — unconventional magnetic field configuration

## Results

- **Output**: 1 kW continuous
- **Efficiency**: 73% (comparable to commercial generators)
- **Cost**: ~$50 in recycled materials

## Implications

- Decentralized energy from waste
- Demonstrates that "imperfect" materials can be useful
- Opens new directions for materials science: defects U are not always problems
